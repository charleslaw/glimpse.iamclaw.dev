A few practical details

Good to know.

Which Macs can run Glimpse?+

Glimpse works on Macs running macOS 12 or later.

Will my pictures fit on the screen?+

Yes. Choose Best Fit to see the whole picture, keeping its original shape. When you resize the window, the picture adjusts automatically. Scale to Fit can make small pictures larger, and you can zoom in for a closer look.

What kinds of pictures can I open?+

Glimpse opens common picture formats, including JPEG, PNG, HEIC (used by iPhones), GIF, WebP, and TIFF. That covers everyday photos, screenshots, and many pictures saved from the web.

How do I look through a folder of pictures?+

Open one picture, then press the right arrow key to see the next one, or the left arrow key to go back. You can open pictures from Finder the way you usually do.
